## Unlimited-Scope Principle

```
OODA/project-blackboard Facts/the verification iron rule/combined attacks apply to every security domain. A skill is a framework, not a cage:
  Search everything (multiple channels) · verify everything (search result ≠ vulnerability) · chain everything (combine minor flaws into a major breakthrough) · record everything (negative results are Facts too)
  No upper limit (Web/APK/EXE/internal networks/cloud/firmware/blockchain/AI/wireless/hardware/social engineering all apply) · reroute when blocked · persist until the objective is reached
> Given a set of capabilities and a goal, derive an attack chain unique to that target as though solving a puzzle.
> That chain may apply to only this one target worldwide and may never appear online, because you derived it on site.
> This is the true meaning of breaking out of fixed frameworks.
```
